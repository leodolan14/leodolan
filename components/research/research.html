Research profile

Research narrative

I completed my PhD in Economic History at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. My research began with labour and living standards in Portuguese Africa before expanding into a comparative study of African colonial economies.

My work combines archival research with quantitative analysis and uses variation across colonial institutions to examine how labour systems shaped workers' living standards and firm performance over time.

The research involved collecting and harmonising archival evidence from nine African and European countries, alongside extended research stays at Lund University, the Paris School of Economics and the University of the Witwatersrand.

  • Two PhD chapters have been published as articles in leading journals.
  • A third article is currently under review.
  • With co-authors, I am developing work on reparations and the economic valuation of colonial injustice.
