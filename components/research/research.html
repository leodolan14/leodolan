Research profile

Research narrative

  1. 01Colonial labour and living standards

    My PhD research examined how colonial labour institutions shaped workers’ living standards and firm performance. It combined archival evidence from nine African and European countries with quantitative analysis to investigate the economic consequences of coercive labour systems and their eventual dismantling.

  2. 02Africa@Work

    At Groningen, I contribute to a reconstruction of occupational change across 33 Sub-Saharan African countries, linking colonial and post-independence evidence from 1920 to 2020.

  3. 03Reparations and colonial injustice

    With Samuel Jones and Peter Gibbon, I am developing research on the economic costs of colonial coercion and on why reparation claims generate different outcomes for states, descendants, and survivors. One paper develops an opportunity-cost framework to quantify the costs of coercion in Portuguese Africa; a second compares contemporary slavery- and colonialism-related reparation cases brought by African states and claimants.
