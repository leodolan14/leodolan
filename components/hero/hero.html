Economic Historian · University of Groningen

Leo Dolan

I am an economic historian specialising in African labour, living standards and long-run economic development. I recently joined the University of Groningen's Faculty of Economics and Business as a Postdoctoral Researcher, working with Professor Jutta Bolt on the ERC-funded Africa @ Work project.

The project reconstructs occupational and employment patterns across 33 Sub-Saharan African countries from 1920 to the present. By linking colonial and post-independence periods, it examines how African labour markets changed, why informal and low-productivity work remain widespread, and how opportunities differed across regions and between men and women.
