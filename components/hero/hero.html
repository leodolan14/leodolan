Leo Dolan

I am an economic historian working on labour, living standards and long-run development in Africa and the Global South. I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Economics, Econometrics and Finance at the University of Groningen, where I work with Professor Jutta Bolt on the ERC-funded Africa@Work project.

My research combines archival evidence with quantitative analysis to examine how colonial institutions and labour systems shaped workers' living standards, firm performance and long-run development.

My PhD in Economic History at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid focused on Portuguese Africa and comparative colonial economies. Two chapters have been published as peer-reviewed articles, and I continue to develop this work through research on reparations and the economic valuation of colonial coercion.

Africa@Work

Africa@Work reconstructs occupational structures across 33 Sub-Saharan African countries from 1920 to 2020. Drawing on historical censuses and labour statistics, the project examines the diverse paths and uneven levels of industrialisation across the continent, tracing long-run shifts in employment between agriculture, manufacturing, services, and other sectors.
